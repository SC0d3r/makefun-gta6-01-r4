VELA CITY is an original three.js open-world crime game: a living city at golden hour, arcade driving, wanted levels and police pursuit, story missions and courier runs, on-foot combat, a minimap and a full HUD.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open the printed local URL, or one of the four shot presets with `?shot=chase`, `?shot=street`, `?shot=drive` or `?shot=mission`.

---

## Controls

WASD moves on foot or drives a car, Shift sprints, Space jumps or pulls the handbrake, F enters or exits a vehicle, E starts a nearby job, LMB fires, R starts the Coral Loop race, Q changes the radio, H mutes audio, and ESC opens the city map. On the title screen, Enter or C continues and N starts a new run. P opens the phone and T opens the stunt board.
