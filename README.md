VELA CITY is an original three.js open-world crime game set in a procedural coastal city at golden hour. Downtown streets hold a crowd of pedestrians and a parked fleet, and the police answer a rising wanted level with cruisers, a helicopter and roadblocks; burning wreck particle effects mark the aftermath of a chase, while story jobs carry you from Palm Row to the marina docks. The landmark skyline brings a Ferris wheel, lighthouse, cargo ship, offshore island, hillside sign and drive-in, all built around arcade driving, on-foot combat, a minimap and a full HUD.

Every game module in this frozen entry carries a single authored note describing its role.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open the printed local URL, or jump straight to one of the four captured frames: `?shot=chase` (Palm Boulevard pursuit), `?shot=street` (downtown sidewalk), `?shot=drive` (marina waterfront run) or `?shot=mission` (marina dock drop).

---

## Controls

WASD moves on foot or drives a car, Shift sprints, Space jumps or pulls the handbrake, F enters or exits a vehicle, E starts a nearby job, LMB fires, R starts the Coral Loop race, Q changes the radio, H mutes audio, and ESC opens the city map. On the title screen, Enter or C continues and N starts a new run. P opens the phone and T opens the stunt board.
